Chipa Recipe

Description

Chipa, or pão de queijo (meaning bread of cheese) is a soft spherical pastry. One can think of it like a "cheese nugget" of sorts. However, no analogy will be sufficient to what a wonderful treat it is.

Chipa are a very common baked good in Argentina and other South American countries. You could walk into any bakery and they'd have some for you or you could go to a grocery store and get a pre-made bag of chipa that you just add water to and bake (and maybe add an egg or two). However, outside of these places we must make due with a more crude form of making these.

Ingredients

  • 0.5kg (~1.1lb) of cassava* starch
  • 3 eggs
  • 150g (~5.3oz) butter
  • 250g (~8.8oz) pategras** cheese
  • 150g (~5.3oz) parmesan** cheese
  • 150g (~5.3oz) milk
  • 1tsp salt
  • 1tsp baking powder

*corn starch is easier to find, but cassava is just better.
**gouda can work too. Its worse but easier to find.

Steps

  1. Cut pategras cheese into small strips or cubes. Shred the parmesan.
  2. In a bowl add the cheeses, cassava starch, and room-temperature butter. Mix by hand.
  3. In another bowl add eggs, salt, baking powder and milk. Mix with a fork.
  4. Pour the bowls together (recommend the liquids into the solids). Mix by hand.
  5. Put mixture into freezer for 30 minutes.
  6. Remove mixture from freezer. Knead into small balls (~golf-ball sized). Put in freezer again for a small while.
  7. Bake at 230°C (446°F) for 10-11 minutes.
  8. Put on the movie Wild Tales (Relatos Salvajes) and enjoy your warm chipa.
