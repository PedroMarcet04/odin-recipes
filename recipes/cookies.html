Cookies Recipe

Description

The cookies my GF makes are amazing. Should take about 1 hour and 45 minutes to make.

Note: the recipe is based on her very poorly organized notes. Some stuff might be wrong due to my interpretation.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup + 1/4 cup + 1.5 tbsp butter
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 0.5 tbsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 egg
  • 0.5 tbsp vanilla
  • 1/6 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2.25 cup + 1/2tbsp flour
  • 7/8 cup powdered sugar
  • 1.5 tbsp milk

Steps

  1. Mix together the following:
    • 1/4 cup butter
    • Brown sugar
    • Ground cinnamon
  2. In a different bowl, mix together the following:
    • 3/4 cup butter
    • Sugar
    • Egg
    • Vanilla
    • Heavy cream
  3. Add and mix (to the second bowl) the following:
    • Baking powder
    • Baking soda
    • Salt
    • Flour
  4. Spread the mixture on a baking sheet. Smear cinnamon mix on top. Chill in fridge for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll up and cut into slices. Ball up the slices. Chill again for 30 minutes.
  6. Bake at 175°C (350°F) for 14 minutes.
  7. (Optional but recommended) Mix together the following:
    • 1.5 tbsp butter
    • Powdered sugar
    • Milk
    You can add this as icing on top of the cookies.
  8. Enjoy! :)
